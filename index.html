Elevation nodes

click the plate to drop a node, shift+click for a basin. drag a node to move it, drag its ring to resize, wheel over it to change height, alt+click to delete. the sliders above edit the selected node — with nothing selected they set the defaults for the next one you place.

Erosion & sea

erosion runs actual droplets over the grid: each one picks up sediment where the slope is steep and drops it where the slope flattens, which is what cuts the branching valleys a pure noise field never has. it is the slowest step — turn it down while you place nodes.

solid body
block plan
3d block hangs the plate on a real camera instead of a shear, so the whole thing is one solid object: shift+drag the sheet to orbit it, shift+wheel to pull in. the body is painted back to front, so with opaque body on, the far flank, the beds of the back wall and the underside are hidden by the ground standing in front of them.
wire mesh
the rungs between one section and the next, hidden by the same horizon the rows use. square cells spaces them to match the rows on screen and ignores the slider.
plate shape
convergence narrows the far rows, depth ease crowds them toward the back, plate turn swings the section line around the map and sample zoom decides how much ground the plate covers.
strata
hatching
the ground under the front of the plate, drawn as beds. flattening takes the deep beds off the shape of the surface and down onto the floor; folding gives each one a warp of its own. shear the plate to swing a flank into view.

Flow

lines follow the downhill gradient, so at 0° they are drainage. rotate toward 90° and they turn to run across the slope instead — the same field, combed the other way.

Hachure

lehmann's rule: strokes point straight downhill and get heavier as the ground steepens, so the flats stay empty paper.

Rivers & paths

rivers come out of the flow accumulation — every cell's water sent to its lowest neighbour, the channels appearing where enough of it has collected. paths are least-cost routes between a shoreline and a summit, and the grade aversion is what makes them break into switchbacks instead of going straight up. plan views only.

Projection

the plan view, taken off the page. globe wraps it round a sphere and shows the near half — wrap decides how much of the world the sheet covers and relief bulge lifts the land off it. relief lays the same drawing on the ground it describes and turns it on the block's camera, with the ground painted underneath, so a contour behind a ridge goes behind the ridge. both orbit with shift+drag, and both use the camera under Profile. plan views only — contour, flow and hachure.

Line work

the same geometry broken into marks along its own arc length, so a contour keeps its path and loses its continuity. dither thins the marks out toward the light end of the ramp, which turns the tone of a line into the density of its dots. every mark is still real geometry and still exports.

Vegetation

cover
symbols scattered where ground, slope and a moisture field allow them: marsh on the wet flats, broadleaf up to the tree line, conifer under it, scrub on what is too steep for either. it rides on the plate as well as the map, so the 3d block comes up wooded.

MIDI

port
scale
the cursor is the instrument. height plays the ground under it on the channel you set; coast plays how far that point lies from the water on the next channel up, rooted on a C — stand in the sea and it is a plain C, walk inland and it climbs the scale.
